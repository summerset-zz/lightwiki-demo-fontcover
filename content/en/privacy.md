## 1. Scope and key points

1. This Policy applies to all services offered on the Site: looking up font information, looking up character coverage, measuring fonts installed on your own machine, saving favourites, and contributing font information.

2. For a quick overview, the key points are set out below. The body of this Policy governs in case of any difference.

(1) There are no accounts on the Site, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email address or identity documents.

(2) We **do not receive or store your font files**. A font is read inside your browser only; the measurements obtained from it may be submitted.

(3) Apart from font measurements, the font's file name and size, the time you gave consent, the monthly code and visit statistics, we collect nothing else.

(4) The Site installs one third-party visit counter (Baidu Analytics) and **no advertising code of any kind**.

## 2. What we collect

We follow the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy and necessity, and collect only the information needed for the functions described below.

1. **Font measurements.** After you tick the consent box on the Contribute page and submit, we receive the family and style names, the measurements, the range of characters covered, and one sample image drawn with that font. None of this contains a font file, and none of it can be used to reconstruct one.

2. **The font's file name and size.** In the same submission your browser reports the name of the file on your device (for example `msyh.ttc`) and its size. This is reference information only and takes no part in any decision.

3. **The monthly code.** Produced on the server at the time of submission, used to avoid counting the same font twice and to limit submission frequency. See section 5.

4. **The time you gave consent.** The moment you ticked the box, kept as a record that we did inform you.

5. **Interface preferences.** When you switch the colour theme or the language, the Site writes cookies in your browser to remember that choice. See section 6.

6. **Visit statistics.** Each time you open a page, Baidu Analytics collects the page address, the referring source, your browser type and your screen size. That information is collected by a third party and **never passes through our server**. See section 4.

7. **Data held in your browser.** Favourites, records of what you have submitted and the results of scanning fonts installed on your machine are kept in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are not uploaded; clearing your browser data deletes them.

## 3. What we never collect

1. Your name, phone number, email address, identity documents or any similar identifying information. The Site has no accounts and no sign-up.

2. **Your font files.** A font is read once inside your browser and released immediately afterwards; it is never uploaded.

3. Your location, the contents of your clipboard, your contacts, or your browsing history on other sites.

4. The Site **installs no advertising code of any kind**. The only external script it loads is Baidu Analytics, for the sole purpose of counting visits.

## 4. Third-party visit statistics (Baidu Analytics)

1. The Site uses Baidu Analytics, provided by Baidu, Inc., to count page views and unique visitors and to understand where visitors come from, so that we can see how each feature is used.

2. The analytics script is served by **Baidu's own servers**. It writes Baidu's own cookies in your browser (all of them are listed in section 6) and obtains your IP address and browser details itself. **We neither receive nor store any of that data**; we only read the aggregates already shown in the Baidu Analytics dashboard.

3. **You may refuse this.** A browser with an ad or tracking blocker will prevent the script from running. Every feature of the Site continues to work; merely that visit is not counted.

4. The script is loaded lazily, takes no part in the first paint and does not slow the page down. In an environment where no analytics identifier is configured (local development, for example) the script is not loaded at all.

## 5. How the "monthly code" is produced

So that we can tell whether a submission comes from the same source as a previous one (the same font should not be counted over and over) without identifying you, we do the following.

1. Your browser generates a random string and keeps it on your device only — in the local storage that browsers set aside for this site, which only this site can read.

2. When you submit, that string is sent to the server, which converts it into a different code using an irreversible algorithm, **discards the original string at once**, and keeps only the code.

3. The key used for that conversion is replaced every month. The same source therefore receives a completely different code each month, and **two different months cannot be linked**.

4. Clearing the Site's data in your browser is equivalent to becoming a new source. The cost is that earlier records can no longer be recognised as belonging to the same source; we accept that cost.

## 7. Our lawful basis for processing

1. Our lawful basis for processing your personal information is **your consent**, under Article 13(1) of the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

2. Without your consent, the Site sends no submission of any kind, including lookups such as whether a particular font is already in the library.

3. You may withdraw your consent at any time:

(1) go back to the Contribute page and untick the box; or

(2) clear the Site's data in your browser ("clear data for this site" / "clear cookies and site data" in your browser settings).

4. After you withdraw consent no new submissions will be made. What has already been submitted cannot be withdrawn record by record, given its nature; see section 9 for how it is handled.

## 8. Where the data is stored and for how long

1. The server is located in **mainland China**. Your information is not transferred outside the country.

2. Retention periods:

(1) Font measurements and monthly codes: retained long term. This font library is the substance of the whole Site; deleting it would leave the library incomplete.

(2) The font's file name, its size and the time you gave consent: retained together with the corresponding font entry.

(3) Server operating logs: kept only briefly while a fault is being investigated, never archived long term, and never used to analyse visitors.

(4) Data in your browser: managed by you and clearable at any time.

3. Once the periods above have elapsed, the corresponding information is deleted or anonymised.

## 9. Your rights

1. Under applicable laws and regulations you have the right to access, copy, correct and delete your personal information, to withdraw your consent, and to ask us to explain our processing.

2. To exercise these rights, contact us by the means given in section 13 and state your request.

3. **A technical limitation we must tell you about.** We hold no identifier that points back to you. The server does not keep the random string from your device, and the monthly code changes every month, so in most cases we cannot go from "a particular user" to "particular records". If you can give concrete clues — the file name at the time of submission, roughly which day — we can use them to locate and delete the matching entries.

4. We will reply within a reasonable period after receiving your request.
